# ARCUS Story Specification: ST-009 (Revised)

## Story Metadata

* **Story ID:** ST-009
* **Story Title:** Analytics, Gamification, and Virtual Odyssey Lab
* **Epic:** Analytics, Habit Insights, and User Motivation
* **App Name:** step-tracker
* **Target Local Environment:** http://localhost:1981
* **Primary Test Target:** Lifetime stats calculation engine, day-of-week and hourly habit pattern visualizations, RPG leveling system ($100\text{ steps} = 1\text{ XP}$), achievement trophy system, and home-base virtual travel progression from Hyderabad.

---

## User Story Statement

> **As a** health tracker user,
> **I want** to analyze my daily walking habits, earn experience points and achievement trophies, and track my lifetime distance on a virtual journey starting from my home base,
> **So that** I remain motivated to stay active without relying on privacy-restricted GPS location tracking.

---

## Context and Business Value

Due to Google Fit's app-isolation privacy restrictions on `com.google.location.sample`, third-party apps cannot reliably fetch historical GPS coordinates recorded by external wearables or mobile apps.

`ST-009` replaces the raw GPS map concept with an offline-first **Analytics, Gamification, and Virtual Odyssey Lab**. By leveraging reliable daily step totals, hourly step buckets (`com.google.step_count.delta` in 1-hour intervals), and cumulative lifetime distance, this story introduces habit analytics, an all-time Hall of Fame, unlockable badges, and a virtual expedition engine.

---

## Scope of Work

1. **Lifetime Hall of Fame & Core Metrics Banner:**
* Calculate and display top-level lifetime metrics derived from all Dexie `daily_records`:
* **Total Lifetime Steps** & **Total Distance (km)**.
* **Lifetime Daily Average Steps**.
* **Longest Active Streak (Days)** matching the currently selected Active Lens.


* **Top 5 All-Time Records Table:** Render a styled leaderboard showing the top 5 highest step days in user history, displaying date, step count, distance, active lens indicator, and link to the screenshot proof modal (if available).


2. **Habit & Pattern Analytics Lab:**
* **Day-of-Week Distribution (Histogram):** A bar chart rendering average steps grouped by day of the week (Monday through Sunday). Highlight the **Power Day** (highest average) and **Lazy Day** (lowest average).
* **24-Hour Peak Stride Chart:** Parse and render 1-hour aggregated step buckets fetched during sync to show step distribution across 24 hours of the day (identifying early bird vs. evening activity spikes).
* **Yearly Monthly Comparison:** Add a year selector dropdown (e.g., 2024, 2025, 2026) rendering a 12-month bar chart highlighting the **Peak Month** (gold badge) and **Lowest Month** (alert badge).


3. **Virtual Expedition Engine (Home Base Navigator):**
* Provide a home base city selector defaulting to **Hyderabad, India** (configurable in settings).
* Define a progressive list of global milestones calculated against cumulative lifetime distance:
* *Hyderabad ➔ Goa* ($650\text{ km}$)
* *Hyderabad ➔ Mumbai* ($710\text{ km}$)
* *Hyderabad ➔ New Delhi* ($1,580\text{ km}$)
* *Hyderabad ➔ Dubai* ($2,890\text{ km}$)
* *Hyderabad ➔ London* ($7,700\text{ km}$)
* *Hyderabad ➔ New York* ($12,500\text{ km}$)


* Render a visual milestone progression bar displaying unlocked routes, current active leg percentage progress, and remaining kilometers.


4. **RPG Leveling & Achievement System:**
* **XP System:** Convert lifetime steps into Experience Points using the formula:

$$\text{XP} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Lifetime Steps}}{100} \right\rfloor$$


* Display player rank/level progression (e.g., *Level 1: Couch Potato* to *Level 50: Globe Trotter*) with an interactive progress bar.
* **Trophy Case (Achievement Badges):** Store and render local unlock status for achievements:
* 🥇 **Centurion:** Walk $>100,000\text{ steps}$ in a single calendar week.
* ⚡ **Marathoner:** Walk $>42.2\text{ km}$ ($>55,000\text{ steps}$) in a single day.
* 🔥 **Unstoppable:** Maintain a 30-day streak above the selected Active Lens.
* 🌙 **Night Owl:** Record $>2,000\text{ steps}$ between 11:00 PM and 3:00 AM.

## Explicit Out of Scope

* Fetching live Google Maps tile APIs or raw GPS coordinate streams.
* Multi-user global leaderboards or online multiplayer competition (covered in future Cloudflare backend specs).

---

## Assumptions

* Hourly step aggregation data is fetched via `dataset:aggregate` with `durationMillis: 3600000` during standard Google Fit sync.
* Existing `daily_records` in IndexedDB can be aggregated client-side without performance degradation.

## Acceptance Criteria (Definition of Done)

### Scenario 1: Lifetime Hall of Fame & Top 5 Days

* **Given** populated step history in Dexie,
* **When** the user opens the "Lab" tab,
* **Then** the UI displays Total Steps, Total Distance, Daily Average, and Longest Streak,
* **And** the Top 5 all-time highest step days are displayed in order with date and step count.

### Scenario 2: Weekday and 24-Hour Habit Insights

* **Given** stored hourly step arrays and daily records,
* **When** viewing the Habit Analytics section,
* **Then** a 7-day bar chart renders the average steps for each weekday, highlighting the highest and lowest days,
* **And** a 24-bar distribution chart renders hourly step patterns across the day.

### Scenario 3: Virtual Expedition Progress from Hyderabad

* **Given** cumulative lifetime distance recorded in the app,
* **When** viewing the Virtual Odyssey section with home base set to Hyderabad,
* **Then** completed milestone legs (e.g., Hyderabad to Goa) display as unlocked,
* **And** the active milestone leg shows an accurate progress percentage based on remaining kilometers.

### Scenario 4: XP Leveling & Achievement Badge Unlock

* **Given** a user completing a step record that satisfies a trophy condition (e.g., single-day step count $>55,000$),
* **When** the database recalculates achievements,
* **Then** the corresponding badge in the Trophy Case transitions from locked to unlocked state,
* **And** the total XP and level rank update immediately.
